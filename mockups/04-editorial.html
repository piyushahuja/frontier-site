01

Manifesto

India keeps only about a fifth of the value of the large enterprises its people build — and almost none of the capital that arrived this past decade reached the frontier of science.

NVIDIA ≈ all of listed India
$4.9 trillion  ≈  $5 trillion
One company now worth about as much as every publicly listed company in India combined · as of July 2026

Long-term ownership capital arrived, plateaued, and came to rent talent — not to own what that talent creates. That is the gap concentrated excellence closes: not many good companies, but a few of genuine global consequence.

A nation that only imports the frontier cannot help steer it. A country that consumes technology built elsewhere is a client, not a partner. Self-reliance — atmanirbharta — was the first step. The frontier company is where self-reliance matures into contribution: to be a friend the world actually needs, India must be a maker at the frontier, not merely a market for it.

Lecture Series

On campus, weekly, through the Monsoon semester. Alumni ten to twenty years ahead return to tell the truth about the decisions they made.

Not placement talks: first-person reflection — what they got right, what they got wrong, what they wish they had known. Convened at IIT Delhi's Department of Computer Science with Prof. Naveen Garg, Head of Department and Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar laureate.
